## 7. Suggested order

1. Re-word the neighbourhood page headings and intro to "houses for sale in {area}" / "land for sale in {area}" (no new pages needed).
2. Add the 10 missing areas in section 2.
3. Add a `/neighborhoods` hub and Colombo 01, 03, 04, 09 to 13, 15.
4. Developer profile titles.
5. District land pages.
6. Special land pages (coconut, commercial, beachfront), only where we hold such listings.
