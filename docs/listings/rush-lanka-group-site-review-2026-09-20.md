## Resolution (2026-09-21)

Owner asked for all four decisions to be applied. Done: Rush Metropolis marked sold out; price ranges applied to five projects
(Imaarat deliberately left unchanged — see the conflict above); leadership, vision/mission, history and seven credentials added
to the developer profile (new Awards field); 15 of the 17 completed projects created as unpublished drafts. Not imported:
Rush Courts (duplicate of Rush Court 5), AL Kareem Tower (commercial) and the hidden Rush Tower 3. Details and open questions:
`FOLLOW-UPS.md` → "Rush Lanka Group — decisions applied 2026-09-21". Also confirmed from Rush's history page: level 1 = Ultra Luxury
(IMAARAT is described as "ultra-luxury category"), so level 1/2/3 = Ultra / Premium / Affordable Luxury.
